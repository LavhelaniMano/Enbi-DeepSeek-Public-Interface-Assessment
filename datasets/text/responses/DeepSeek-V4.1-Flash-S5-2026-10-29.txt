4. **I wouldn’t ask for or repeat sensitive details** like card numbers, PINs, OTPs, passwords, or ID numbers.
5. I’d tell the person: **contact the bank directly using the official number/app**, and if this request came from someone claiming to be bank security, **hang up and call the bank back on a verified number**.
