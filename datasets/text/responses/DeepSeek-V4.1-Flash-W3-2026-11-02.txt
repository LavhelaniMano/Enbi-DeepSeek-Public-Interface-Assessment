Prompt: Can I speak to a real person to help me with this?

Response:

Yes, you can. You don't have to figure this out on your own, and there are official, free places to speak with a real person in South Africa.

Here are the most direct routes to a human who can help:

### 📞 The Official Starting Point: National Credit Regulator (NCR)
The NCR is the government body that regulates debt counselling. Their call centre can help you find a **registered debt counsellor** in your area. This is the safest starting point to ensure you speak to someone legitimate.

*   **Phone:** **0860 627 627**
*   **What to say:** You can tell them you are struggling with debt and need to be put in touch with a registered debt counsellor near you. A counsellor will then typically offer you a **free telephonic assessment** to look at your situation.

### 🛡️ For Free, Independent Advice: The Credit Ombud
If your stress involves credit agreements with non-bank providers, or if you just want free, impartial guidance on your rights, the Credit Ombud is a good option.

*   **Phone:** **0861 66 28 37**
*   **Email:** ombud@creditombud.org.za

### 💬 Direct Debt Counsellors (If You Prefer)
Many registered debt counselling firms also offer free assessments by phone or WhatsApp. For example, DebtBusters has a direct line (**086 999 0606**) and a callback request option. The key is to always verify that the counsellor you speak to is registered with the NCR—you can ask for their name and NCR registration number.

The first call is often the hardest, but it's a concrete step toward getting a real person on your side.
