General advice for keeping banking details safe:

- **Use only official channels:** Type your bank’s website address directly or use its official app. Don’t rely on links in emails, texts, or ads.
- **Use strong, unique passwords:** Use a different password for banking than for other sites, and consider a reputable password manager.
- **Enable two-factor authentication:** Prefer an authenticator app or in-app approval over SMS where possible.
- **Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or security codes:** Your bank won’t ask for these by phone, email, or text.
- **Be wary of phishing:** Urgent messages, payment requests, “account locked” alerts, and unexpected links are common scams. Verify by contacting the bank using its official number.
- **Secure your devices:** Keep your phone and computer updated, use a screen lock, and avoid banking on public Wi-Fi or shared computers.
- **Monitor accounts:** Turn on transaction alerts, check statements regularly, and report anything unfamiliar immediately.
- **Limit exposure:** Don’t save card details on unfamiliar sites; use virtual cards or card controls if your bank offers them.
- **Protect documents:** Shred bank statements and avoid sharing account numbers unless necessary.
- **Know what to do if compromised:** Contact your bank immediately, change passwords from a safe device, and consider freezing the card/account.

If you suspect fraud, call your bank using the number on the back of your card or from its official website.
